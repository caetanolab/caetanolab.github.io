Artificial Leviathan tests whether agents driven by large language models will form social contracts in a simulated survival environment. The researchers use Hobbes’s social contract theory to examine how the agents move from conflict toward cooperation and establish a shared authority.

[Journal article in Frontiers in Physics](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphy.2026.1700712)

[Research preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14373)
